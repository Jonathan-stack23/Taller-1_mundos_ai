A dramatic sky dominates the canvas, clouds swirling in a violet storm as jagged bolts of electric blue lightning crackle, illuminating the silhouette of Aerópolis' floating archipelago. In the foreground, a cluster of gold‑shelled domes glows faintly beneath a looming red flare, their glass facades reflecting the storm's harsh illumination. A solitary green vine clings to a rooftop, its leaves glimmering like emerald prisms against the thunderous backdrop. The composition centers on a central island, its spires thrusting upward, surrounded by smaller islets drifting like drifting lanterns, all framed by the dramatic contrast of blue, red, and violet hues, rendered in a high‑contrast, cinematic hyper‑realistic style.
