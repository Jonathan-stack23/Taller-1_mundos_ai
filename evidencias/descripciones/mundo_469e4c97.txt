A vast expanse of crystalline dunes glistens beneath a low, eclipsed moon, its silvery surface now tinged with the shadow of the Earth. The desert floor is a mosaic of translucent glass tiles reflecting a turquoise glow, while black silhouettes of jagged dunes cut the horizon. In the center, the moon, veiled in a thin magenta ring, casts an eerie orange halo that ripples across the mirrored sand. Light is diffused, giving the whole scene a dreamlike, almost surreal quality, and the composition draws the eye from the textured dunes, through the glowing sky, to the eclipse's haunting edge.
